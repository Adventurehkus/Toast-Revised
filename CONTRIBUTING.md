# 🤝 Contributing to Toast Revised

First off, thank you for wanting to help make Toast Revised the gold standard for clean, elite-tier Minecraft optimization! To keep our codebase beautifully organized, our FPS maximized, and our files entirely safe, all contributors are expected to follow these guidelines.

---

## 🗺️ How You Can Help

You don't have to be a developer to contribute! We welcome help in several areas:
1. **Bug Reporting:** Finding performance leaks, broken keybinds, or mod conflicts.
2. **Feature & Mod Ideas:** Suggesting lightweight performance or Vanilla+ mods (always do this in our **GitHub Discussions** tab, not Issues!).
3. **Documentation:** Fixing typos or adding optimization guides to our GitHub Wiki.

---

## 🐛 Submitting Pull Requests (PRs)

If you are changing configuration files, optimizing scripts, or updating mod manifests, please follow this workflow:

### 1. File & Optimization Rules
* **No Bloat:** We strictly maintain a lightweight, performance-first philosophy. Any mod or configuration change must respect our memory headroom and frame rate goals.
* **Vanilla+ Harmony:** Changes should not disrupt the classic vanilla feel or break our custom dark theme menus.
* **No Piracy or Hacks:** We will instantly reject and close any pull requests containing cracked files, blacklisted clients, or bypassed authentication tools.

### 2. The PR Checklist
Before you hit submit on your PR, make sure you have:
* [ ] Forked the repository and created your branch from the latest commit.
* [ ] Tested the configuration changes locally on your own client to ensure the game boots cleanly.
* [ ] Included a brief explanation of *why* this change is beneficial to the pack's performance or gameplay.
* [ ] Confirmed that your modified files do not overwrite user-specific settings (like custom player names or private server IPs).

---

## 📜 Code of Conduct

By participating in this project, you agree to abide by our official **Community Code of Conduct**. Keep all interactions friendly, constructive, and focused on building an amazing gameplay experience for everyone.

Thank you for helping optimize the ultimate Vanilla+ pack! 🍞🚀
