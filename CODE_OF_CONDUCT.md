### 🌟 Toast Revised: Community Guidelines

**Our Philosophy**
Toast Revised is all about creating a polished, seamless experience, and we want our community spaces to reflect that same level of quality. We believe that a great modpack deserves an equally great community. Whether you are here to report a bug, suggest a feature, or help out another player, we expect everyone to keep interactions friendly, constructive, and respectful.

---

### 👋 The Behavior We Encourage

We want this repository to be a productive environment where everyone feels comfortable participating. Please keep these principles in mind:

* **Lead with Empathy:** Treat every player and contributor with patience and kindness, no matter their background, identity, or level of technical experience.
* **Focus on Solutions:** When giving feedback or discussing a bug, keep it constructive. Focus on what makes the pack better for the collective player base.
* **Act with Integrity:** If a misunderstanding happens, own up to it, apologize, and keep moving forward. We value collaboration over ego.

---

### 🛑 Where We Draw the Line

To protect our community, certain behaviors are strictly prohibited. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for actions that disrupt the peace of our project spaces:

* **Harassment & Attacks:** Personal insults, derogatory remarks, political arguments, and any form of public or private targeted harassment.
* **Inappropriate Content:** Using sexualized language, posting offensive imagery, spamming the issue tracker, or being demanding toward developers.
* **Privacy Breaches:** Sharing anyone’s private, real-world information (like names or contact details) without explicit, verified permission.

---

### 🛡️ Moderation & Reporting

These guidelines apply across all of our official spaces, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Wiki pages, and Project Discussions.

Project leadership is responsible for keeping these spaces healthy. We reserve the right to edit, lock, or delete any comments or contributions that violate these rules. Severe or repeated violations will result in a permanent ban from the repository.

If you witness someone breaking these guidelines, please flag the comment or report it privately to the project leaders so we can handle it promptly and fairly.
